# 01 — Product Vision

## 1. Product name

**CreatorOS**

## 2. Tagline

**Plan. Execute. Publish. Grow.**

## 3. Vision statement

CreatorOS gives solo creators a practical operating system for managing their entire content workflow—from idea selection to production, publishing, repurposing, and review—without requiring a full software platform or an operations team.

## 4. Mission

Help solo creators publish consistently by converting strategy into daily execution.

## 5. Core problem

Most creators do not fail because they lack ideas. They fail because their content operation is fragmented.

Typical failure points:

- Ideas are scattered across notes, chats, and documents.
- Publishing dates are planned without production tasks.
- Content volume exceeds available time.
- Missed tasks are not recovered intelligently.
- Long-form content is not repurposed systematically.
- Creators do not know what to do today.
- Analytics are recorded but not translated into decisions.
- Existing planners are static and require manual maintenance.
- Existing SaaS platforms are often too expensive or too complex for early-stage creators.

## 6. Product promise

CreatorOS tells the creator:

1. What to create.
2. What to do today.
3. What is at risk.
4. What was missed.
5. What to publish next.
6. What to repurpose.
7. What to change based on results.

## 7. Target customer

### Primary customer

Solo creators who use one primary platform and one or more secondary distribution platforms.

Examples:

- YouTube creator repurposing content to Shorts, LinkedIn, Instagram, or newsletters.
- Consultant using LinkedIn and a newsletter.
- Podcaster turning episodes into clips and posts.
- Educational creator producing weekly long-form and short-form content.

### Initial recommended niche

YouTube-led creators publishing across multiple platforms.

## 8. Jobs to be done

### Functional jobs

- Build a realistic publishing plan.
- Break content items into production tasks.
- Schedule work based on available capacity.
- Push approved tasks to Google Calendar.
- Track task and content status.
- Recover overdue work.
- Repurpose long-form content.
- Review execution and performance.

### Emotional jobs

- Reduce uncertainty.
- Reduce guilt caused by missed publishing.
- Create a sense of control.
- Make progress visible.
- Replace inconsistent motivation with a repeatable operating rhythm.

## 9. Product principles

### 9.1 Execution over decoration

CreatorOS is not a visually attractive calendar with limited operational value. Its primary purpose is execution.

### 9.2 Capacity before ambition

The system must never recommend a plan that exceeds the creator’s stated available time without an explicit warning.

### 9.3 Production before publishing

Every publishing commitment must have upstream production tasks.

### 9.4 Recovery over punishment

Missed tasks should trigger recovery choices, not only overdue indicators.

### 9.5 One source of truth

The Google Sheet is the authoritative record for ideas, content items, tasks, calendar links, status, and metrics.

### 9.6 AI is optional

Core planning and workflow features must work without an AI provider.

### 9.7 Customer-funded AI

When AI is enabled, the customer provides and funds their own API key.

### 9.8 Human approval

AI suggestions must never automatically create a final publishing commitment without user approval.

## 10. Differentiation

CreatorOS differentiates from generic content planners through:

- Workload-aware planning.
- Production-task decomposition.
- Google Calendar push.
- Missed-task recovery.
- Repurposing workflows.
- Daily priority recommendations.
- Optional AI suggestions.
- Customer-owned API usage.
- One-time purchase model.

## 11. Non-goals for v1

CreatorOS v1 will not:

- Publish directly to social platforms.
- Import live analytics through social APIs.
- Host user accounts.
- Support teams or approvals.
- Operate as a subscription SaaS.
- Generate complete videos or designs.
- Store the seller’s AI API key.
- Guarantee growth or platform performance.

## 12. Long-term direction

If demand is validated, CreatorOS can evolve into a web application with:

- User accounts.
- Native notifications.
- Social-platform integrations.
- Automated analytics import.
- Team workspaces.
- AI recommendation memory.
- Subscription billing.
- Agency and client management.

The v1 architecture should remain clean enough to allow later migration, but migration readiness must not expand v1 scope.
